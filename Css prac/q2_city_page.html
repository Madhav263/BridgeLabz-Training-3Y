Jaipur - The Pink City

History Food Places Travel

History

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II and is the capital of Rajasthan.

Food

Try dal baati churma, ghewar and the famous pyaaz kachori from local markets.

Places to Visit

Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar are must-see places.

Best Time to Visit

October to March has pleasant weather and is the best time for sightseeing.
