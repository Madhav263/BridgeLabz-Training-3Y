City Launches New Green Park Project

The city council announced a new green park project on Monday, aiming to bring more open space to crowded neighbourhoods. Officials said work will begin early next year and will be completed in phases.

The park will include walking trails, a children's play area and a small lake. Local residents have welcomed the plan and hope it will improve air quality and give families a safe place to relax.

Environmental experts say that urban parks reduce heat and support local wildlife. They advised the council to plant native trees and use rainwater harvesting to keep the park green throughout the year.

Shopkeepers nearby expect more visitors once the park opens. A weekend market is also being planned beside the main entrance, giving small vendors a chance to sell fresh produce and handmade goods.

Citizens can share their suggestions on the design by visiting the municipal office or sending an email before the end of the month. The council promised to review every idea before finalising the plan.

The project is expected to cost several crores and will be funded jointly by the city and state governments. Officials said regular updates will be published as work moves forward.
